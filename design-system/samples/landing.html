02 How it works

Four stops through the boiler room

Your URL goes in one end. A launch video comes out the other. You step in once.

Step 1

Research

The Researcher reads your site and copies every claim word for word, with its source.

Step 2

Script

The Director writes the scenes. The Validator sends back any number your site doesn't back up.

Step 3 · you

You approve

Read the script and the source behind each number. Nothing renders until you say yes.

Step 4

Animate and deliver

Scenes, voice and music come together. Quality Check reviews every frame before you get it.

04 The crew

Your launch team, minus the hiring

Each agent has one job and makes its own calls. You can see every decision they make.

Researcher

Collects screenshots, colours, fonts and claims from your site.

Decides: what's worth saying

Director

Writes the script and times every scene to the frame.

Decides: the story

Validator

Blocks made-up numbers, bad timing and hard-to-read text.

Decides: pass or send back

Art Director

Picks a motion template for each scene and makes what's missing.

Decides: how it looks

Quality Check

Watches every transition and reads every line at phone size.

Decides: ship or redo

Account Manager

Delivers your files and handles your revisions.

Decides: what to send back
